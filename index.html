The Vision & The Spark

During my engineering coursework at Symbiosis Skills and Professional University, I noticed a recurring gap: students understood cloud computing theory, but were hesitant to create actual AWS accounts, configure VPC subnets, or deploy serverless functions due to fear of billing spikes or architectural complexity.

I founded the **AWS Student Builder Group @ SSPU** to eliminate that barrier. We established a hands-on sandbox where every member deploys production-grade architectures directly in Computer Lab 3.

Key Milestones

1

University Chapter Recognition & Lab Allocation

Formal approval secured from the SSPU School of Data Science for dedicated sessions in Computer Lab 3.

2

Cloud Practitioner & SAA-C03 Bootcamps (100% Hands-on)

Onboarded 148 verified students across CSIT and Cybersecurity with zero billing incidents.

3

Digital Pass & Attendance Kiosk Deployment

Developed internal community tools including the QR Attendance System, Certificate Portal, and Builder Pass generator.

"Our philosophy is simple: don't just consume cloud slides. Architect them, stress-test them, secure them, and break them in sandboxes. You are a builder from day one."
— Disha Pure, Founder
